%--------------------------ABSTRACT---------------------------%
\input{metafiles/abstractpage.tex}
\makeabstract{%
Reinforcement learning (RL) has shown strong performance in simulation, but applying RL to real-world tasks remains challenging due to environmental uncertainty, high-dimensionality, data collection, and safety considerations. Incorporating strategies from robust, offline, and model-based RL, this work proposes a policy learning framework for contextual bandit settings to tackle the many challenges of real-world RL. An initial data collection strategy is implemented to promote sample diversity. An ensemble of reward networks are trained on the data and used to predict the reward and to penalize uncertain estimates. A new objective called the perturbed action robustness (PAR) objective is introduced, encouraging selection of actions that achieve high reward and remain stable under perturbations. The method further incorporates iterative deployment and data aggregation to continuously refine the reward model and policy. Experimental results in a custom Gym environment and a robotic grasping task demonstrate improved performance compared to baseline methods. 
}%
